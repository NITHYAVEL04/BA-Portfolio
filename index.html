Case Study 1: Reducing Operational Alert Fatigue via Dynamic Anomaly Detection

AIOps & Incident Management

Business Problem: Fixed CPU alert thresholds (e.g., 80% limit) caused false-positive alert fatigue during normal peak business hours while missing abnormal off-peak spikes, slowing down IT incident triage.

Stakeholder & Root Cause Analysis

Stakeholders: IT Operations Responders, Engineering Leads, SLA Owners.
Root Cause: Static rules ignored cyclical workload patterns and required manual threshold updates.

To-Be Solution & Trade-off

Implemented baseline-learning anomaly detection (AWS CloudWatch Anomaly Detection, Lambda, SNS). Chose managed cloud detection over custom ML models to reduce maintenance overhead.

Process Flow Comparison: As-Is vs. To-Be Alerting
